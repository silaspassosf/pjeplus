## iLovePDF Desktop 2.0.178

06/05/2024

<br/>

### Improvements

- The whole Reader module has been reviewed and redefined. The following are some of the most notable improvements:
    - Scroll behaviour has been improved to ease readability.
    - Now reader has better compatibility to open and read much more PDF documents.
    - Zooming rendering has been improved.
    - Several issues affecting app stability and performance have been fixed.
- Added Single Sign On as a new login option.
- Fixed an issue for which the app could crash  in Google or Facebook login attemps.
