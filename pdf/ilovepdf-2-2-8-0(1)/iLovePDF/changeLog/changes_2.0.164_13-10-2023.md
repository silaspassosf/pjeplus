## iLovePDF Desktop 2.0.164

13/10/2023

<br/>

### New tools

- Sign PDF: You can now e-Sign your PDF documents in a few simple steps.
- Scan to PDF: Scan documents using your mobile device and send them instantly to your iLovePDF Desktop App.

<br/>

### Improvements

- You can now download and manage iLovePDF Desktop on the Apple Store.
- Reader: Several improvements made to enhance the smoothness of zooming and scrolling.
- Reader: Several improvements that optimize performance.
- Reader: Users can now select Right to Left or Left to Right lecture when selecting two-page viewing mode.
- Reader now accepts WebP image files.
- Languages: Several fixes and improvements affecting UI and tools in Arabic.
- OCR PDF: Improvements made to conversion speed and text detection.
